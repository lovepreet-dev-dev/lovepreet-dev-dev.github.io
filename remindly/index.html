🔔 Remindly

Reminders & Alarms for Android · by Lovepreet Sharma

Remindly helps you do your daily habits on time: lunch at 1:30, medicine at 9, water every two hours, rent on the 1st. It rings at the right minute, and if you don't answer, it asks again. Tick Done, snooze it, or skip it.

What it does

  • Type a reminder the way you'd say it: "Drink water every 2 hours", "Pay rent every month on the 1st".
  • 100 ready-made reminders for meals, water, medicine, fitness, sleep, bills and family.
  • Flexible repeats: every few hours, daily, chosen weekdays, monthly or yearly.
  • An alarm-style card with Complete, Snooze and Skip, even on the lock screen.
  • Streaks for every habit, a calendar of the days you did it, and a home-screen widget.
  • Works fully offline. No account needed, no ads, no tracking.

Google sign-in and Drive backup (optional)

You can sign in with your Google account to back up your reminders to your own Google Drive and restore them on a new phone. Remindly asks only for access to its own hidden app folder in your Drive (the drive.appdata scope); it can't see any other file. The backup stays in your Drive, and the developer can't read it. Your name and email are used only to keep you signed in. You can sign out or delete your account and backup from the app at any time.
